## 1. What we collect

**Account data** (when you register): display name, username, email
address, a hashed password (we never store your plaintext password),
and a timestamp of when you agreed to these policies.

**Age confirmation**: we store only a boolean confirmation that you
meet the minimum age requirement and the date you confirmed it — we
do not collect or store your birthdate.

**Content you create**: series, chapters, comments, and reviews you
publish, and any images you upload for covers or comic pages.

**Usage data**: your reading progress (which chapter, scroll
position or page number) per series, your saved library, chapters
you've marked for offline reading, and notifications generated by
activity on series you follow or comment on.

**Technical data**: standard server request logs (IP address,
timestamp, endpoint) generated by the web server for security and
debugging, retained for [N days].

## 2. What we do not collect

We do not collect payment information (the current implementation has
no payment processing), government ID numbers, or precise location
data.

## 3. How we use your data

- To operate your account (authentication, session management).
- To display your published content to other users.
- To sync your reading progress and library across sessions.
- To send you in-app notifications about series you follow.
- To respond to DMCA takedown notices or Terms of Service violations.

## 4. How we share your data

- Your username, display name, and published content are visible to
  other users by design (this is a public reading platform).
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We may disclose data if required by law, or to respond to a valid
  legal process (subpoena, court order).
