## For creators publishing on Vorth

Before you publish a series or chapter, you're asked to confirm that
you own the rights to it, or have permission to publish it. Concretely,
that means:

- **Original work**: If you wrote the novel or drew the comic
  yourself, you're the copyright holder and you're fine to publish.
- **Collaborative work**: If you co-wrote or co-illustrated a work,
  make sure you have your collaborators' agreement before publishing
  it under one account (Vorth only allows the publishing account to
  add chapters — see Terms of Service §4).
- **Licensed work**: If you're publishing a translation, adaptation,
  or a work under license from someone else, you need to have that
  license in hand, and you're responsible for complying with its
  terms (attribution, revenue share, territory restrictions, etc.).
- **Fan works**: Fan fiction and fan art occupy a legally gray area
  that varies by original work and jurisdiction. Publishing it is at
  your own risk, and a rights holder can still file a valid takedown
  notice against it.
- **AI-generated content**: If any part of your submission was
  AI-generated, be aware that copyright ownership of AI-generated
  content is an unsettled and jurisdiction-dependent legal question.

## What happens if you publish something you don't have rights to

- The rights holder can file a DMCA takedown notice (see DMCA
  Policy), which will result in the content being removed.
- Repeat infringement can result in account termination.
- You may be personally liable to the rights holder independent of
  anything Vorth does — removing the content does not resolve any
  claim between you and the rights holder.

## For readers

- Content on Vorth is protected by copyright, owned by its
  creators. Downloading a chapter for offline reading within the app
  is permitted; redistributing, reposting, or republishing content
  from Vorth elsewhere without the creator's permission is not.
- If you believe something on Vorth infringes your copyright, see the
  DMCA Policy for how to file a notice.
